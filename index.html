OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Motorcycle Maintenance

Sample service menu tailored for local commuting, touring, and everyday riding conditions.

General Motorcycle Servicing

Complete multi-point checkup, nut & bolt tightening, cable lubrication, air filter cleanup, and overall road-readiness check.

Engine Diagnostics & Overhaul

Accurate identification of engine noise, loss of compression, valve clearances, piston ring inspection, and full engine rebuilds.

Carburetor & Fuel Tuning

Precision carburetor jet cleaning, idling adjustment, fuel mixture balancing, and throttle response restoration for smooth performance.

Brake System Service

Disc brake pad replacement, drum shoe fitting, master cylinder bleed, brake fluid flush, and responsive lever adjustment.

Clutch & Transmission Repair

Clutch plate replacement, friction disk service, gear shift assembly adjustment, and smooth gear engagement troubleshooting.

Electrical & Battery Check

Headlight, indicator, horn wiring repairs, battery voltage test, stator coil inspection, fuse replacement, and starter motor service.

Chain & Sprocket Service

Drive chain cleaning, degreasing, high-viscosity lubrication, slack adjustment, and front/rear sprocket wear checks.

Tyre & Wheel Care

Tubeless puncture repair, tyre replacement fitting, spoke lacing adjustment, wheel truing, and tire pressure calibration.

Suspension & Fork Overhaul

Front shock fork oil replacement, fork seal replacement, rear shock absorber check, and stiffness balancing for rough roads.

Engine Oil & Filter Change

Drain and refill with high-grade engine oil suited to your bike manufacturer specifications, plus oil filter & strainer cleaning.

Pre-Trip & Highway Inspection

Full mechanical checklist before long highway rides across Nepal to prevent unexpected breakdowns on mountain routes.

Custom Adjustments & Repairs

Tailored mechanical troubleshooting, handlebar alignment, crash guard fitting, and custom practical motorcycle modifications.

WORKFLOW

Our 5-Step Service Process

A systematic mechanical workflow ensuring thorough inspection and reliable delivery.

01

Bike Inspection

Initial physical assessment of the motorcycle, listening to rider concerns and checking general condition.

02

Problem Diagnosis

Systematic mechanical check of engine, brakes, electricals, or drive chain to isolate the exact cause.

03

Service / Repair

Executing necessary maintenance, part adjustments, oil replacement, or component repairs with precision tools.

04

Final Check

Comprehensive test drive, safety verification, brake responsiveness test, and bolt tightening re-check.

05

Delivery & Advice

Handing over the serviced motorcycle with maintenance recommendations and honest care tips.
